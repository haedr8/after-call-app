# Store listing (English)

**App name:** Haulni – Phone & WhatsApp replies

**Short description (80):** A phone app with ready WhatsApp replies after every call.

**Full description:**
Haulni is a full phone app built for drivers, couriers and anyone who calls many customers.

Calling:
• Dialpad, call log and contacts with search
• In-call screen: answer, decline, end, mute, speaker, Bluetooth, hold, two calls
• Caller names from your contacts

WhatsApp replies after calls:
• When a call ends, a notification shows the number
• Pick a ready message such as "I arrived" or "On my way" and WhatsApp opens with the text filled in
• Tap a number inside another app to get quick options: call, WhatsApp call, ready messages, my location, note
• Editable templates, notes per number, backup
• Works with WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business

My expenses:
• Track your salary and spending by category (fuel, maintenance, rent, food…) and see where your money went
• Personal debt tracker: money you borrowed or lent, with repayments
• Daily and weekly stats; data stays on your phone

Privacy:
• Sends no data anywhere; the app has no internet permission
• Reads call log and contacts only to display them in the app
• Does not record calls
• No ads, no account

To use it, set Haulni as your default phone app (you can switch back any time). Not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
