# Store listing (English)

**App name:** Haulni – After-call replies

**Short description (80):** Pick a ready WhatsApp message right after every call.

**Full description:**
Haulni is a simple helper for drivers, couriers and anyone who calls many customers.

When a call ends, a small notification appears. Tap it, choose a ready message such as "I have arrived" or "I'm on my way", and WhatsApp opens with the text filled in.

Features:
• Notification after a call ends (incoming, outgoing, missed)
• Editable quick-reply templates
• Enter a number or pick a contact with the system picker
• Share your current location by WhatsApp message
• Private notes per number
• Works with WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business
• Template backup

Privacy:
• No data is sent anywhere; everything stays on your phone
• Does not read call log, contacts or messages
• Does not record calls
• No ads, no account

Uses the "Phone state" permission only to detect when a call ends. Not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
